COGY Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

COGY ("we", "us") builds automated WhatsApp assistants that help small businesses answer customer messages and take bookings. This page explains what information we collect when you message us on WhatsApp, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you send a message to the COGY WhatsApp number, we receive:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name
  • The content of the messages you send us
  • Your answers to our questions, such as the type of business you run and what your customers ask about most

We do not ask for passwords, payment card details, or government ID numbers through WhatsApp.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your messages and run our automated conversation
  • To prepare a demo or quote for your business and follow up with you personally
  • To improve our service

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Services that process your information

Your messages travel through the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. We also use an automation tool (n8n) to run our replies and store your answers so we can follow up. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as we need it to respond to you and provide our service, or as required by law.

5. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. You can also stop the conversation at any time by telling us or blocking the number. To make a request, contact us at the email below.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
